## Bundled typing-practice texts

The package bundles **eight** short texts, two per language (Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese):

| ID | Language | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `ko-n01` · `en-n01` · `zh-n01` · `ja-n01` | ko / en / zh / ja | A short new story, one per language. |
| `ko-g01` · `en-g01` · `zh-g01` · `ja-g01` | ko / en / zh / ja | A typing text that explains how to add your own story file. |

**How they were written.** Written on 2026-11-26 by Claude (an AI model made by Anthropic) at the request of the HoloKeyboard project owner, for this project. The story plots are newly invented. They are not based on, copied from or adapted from any existing tale, book, song, game or translation, and no existing text was consulted while writing them. The guide texts describe HoloKeyboard's own feature. This is the project's own account of how the texts were made. It is not an independent originality check and not legal advice.

## Your own story files

Story files that you create yourself in your personal folder (see the user guide, "Add your own story") are for **your personal use** on your PC. The program only reads them locally; it never uploads them. Text written by other people (books, songs, websites, translations) remains under its authors' copyright: typing it for private practice is your own matter, but sharing or redistributing it (including adding it to a repository or package) needs the rights holder's permission.
